# Tic-Tac-Toe

## Overview
Two players take turns marking spaces in a 3x3 grid. The first player to get three marks in a row wins.

## Rules
1. Player 1 uses X, Player 2 uses O
2. Players alternate turns, starting with Player 1
3. On your turn, click an empty cell to place your mark
4. The game ends when:
   - A player gets three marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line (that player wins)
   - All nine cells are filled with no winner (draw)
