# Rock Paper Scissors

## Overview
A classic hand game played between two players. Each round, both players simultaneously choose rock, paper, or scissors.

## Rules
1. Both players choose their move secretly (the opponent won't see until both have chosen)
2. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock
3. If both players choose the same move, the round is a tie
4. First player to win 2 rounds wins the match
